BabyNova Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

BabyNova is a newborn care tracking app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information You Provide

BabyNova lets you record baby profile information and care logs, including feeding, jaundice values, weight, diaper records, sleep records, reminders, and notes you choose to enter.

How Information Is Used

The app uses your records to show summaries, trends, reminders, Home Screen widgets, and AI-powered care summaries. BabyNova is not a medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice tool and does not replace a qualified healthcare professional.

Storage and Processing

Care records are stored on your device. BabyNova sends data to the AI service only when you choose to generate an AI-powered care summary and agree to the in-app data sharing notice.

AI Care Summaries

When you request an AI care summary, BabyNova may send the selected baby's profile information, recent feeding records, jaundice records, weight records, app language, region, and related care notes to the BabyNova AI service. The BabyNova AI service may use configured AI providers, such as DeepSeek, Claude, or OpenAI, to generate the requested summary.

This information is used only to provide the AI summary you requested. AI-generated content is for log organization and general information only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Tracking and Advertising

BabyNova does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes. BabyNova does not use advertising SDKs.

Children's Privacy

BabyNova is intended for parents and caregivers. The app may contain information about a baby entered by a parent or caregiver. Please do not enter information unless you have the right to do so.

Data Deletion

You can delete records from within the app. For privacy questions or deletion requests related to server-side processing, contact us at 68183830@qq.com.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email 68183830@qq.com.
