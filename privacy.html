小守护 / BabyNova 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 8 月 29 日

小守护 / BabyNova 是面向父母和照护者的新生儿护理记录工具。本政策说明应用会处理哪些信息、如何使用这些信息，以及你可以如何联系我们。

应用提供的黄疸、喂养、体重、尿布、睡眠等提示仅用于记录整理和一般护理参考，不构成医疗诊断、治疗或医疗建议。如宝宝出现精神差、吃奶明显减少、黄疸加重、尿布明显减少、白便、血便等异常情况，请及时咨询专业医生。

我们处理的信息

你可以在应用中主动记录宝宝资料和护理记录，包括宝宝昵称或姓名、出生日期、性别、出生体重、孕周、喂养、黄疸数值、体重、尿布、睡眠、提醒设置和备注内容。

华为应用市场 Android 首发版本以本地记录为主，不提供账号系统，不接入广告 SDK，不接入第三方统计 SDK，不请求定位、通讯录、相机、麦克风、照片、运动、健康数据等系统权限。

信息的使用方式

  • 展示首页概览、记录列表、趋势和护理提示。
  • 在你设置提醒时，通过本地通知提示喂养、黄疸或体重记录。
  • 在 iOS 版本中，用于本地小组件展示摘要。
  • 在你主动请求 AI 护理摘要时，用于生成你请求的摘要内容。

本地存储与网络处理

Android 华为首发版本的护理记录保存在你的设备本地。卸载应用或清除应用数据会删除本地数据。

iOS 版本的护理记录主要保存在设备本地，主屏幕小组件摘要通过本地 App Group 存储读取。iOS 版本的订阅购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理，我们不会接收你的银行卡号或完整支付凭证。

AI 护理摘要

在支持 AI 护理摘要的版本中，只有当你主动点击生成摘要并同意应用内提示时，应用才会发送所选宝宝的资料和相关护理记录给 BabyNova AI 服务。BabyNova AI 服务可能使用配置的 AI 服务提供方生成摘要，例如 DeepSeek、Claude 或 OpenAI。

AI 生成内容仅用于记录整理和一般信息参考，不能替代专业医生。请不要把 AI 摘要作为诊断、治疗或用药依据。

广告、分析与追踪

iOS 版本使用 PostHog 和 AppsFlyer Strict（无 IDFA 版本）了解应用及订阅流程是否正常。应用可能发送匿名事件，例如应用打开、付费墙浏览、所选订阅商品标识符、购买或恢复结果、商品加载结果及订阅权益状态；事件还可能包含应用版本、构建号、平台和 Apple App 标识符。

这些分析事件不会包含宝宝资料、喂养、黄疸、体重、尿布、睡眠记录、护理备注、AI 输入或结果。会话录屏及自动页面、控件采集均已关闭。我们不会为了广告目的跨应用或跨网站追踪你，也不会收集 IDFA。Android 华为首发版本仍不接入第三方统计 SDK。

儿童隐私

小守护 / BabyNova 面向父母和照护者使用，不面向儿童独立使用。请仅在你有权记录相关信息的情况下录入宝宝资料和护理记录。

数据删除与权利请求

你可以在应用内删除记录，也可以通过卸载应用或清除应用数据删除本地保存的信息。对于可能涉及服务器处理的 AI 摘要相关问题、隐私咨询或删除请求，请通过以下邮箱联系我们。

BabyNova Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

BabyNova is a newborn care tracking app for parents and caregivers. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

BabyNova is for care tracking and general informational support only. It is not a medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice tool and does not replace a qualified healthcare professional.

Information You Provide

You may enter baby profile information and care logs, including name or nickname, birth date, gender, birth weight, gestational age, feeding, jaundice values, weight, diaper records, sleep records, reminders, and notes.

How Information Is Used

The app uses your records to show summaries, trends, reminders, Home Screen widgets on iOS, and care tips. In versions that support AI care summaries, data is sent to the BabyNova AI service only when you request an AI summary and agree to the in-app notice.

Storage and Processing

The first Android release for Huawei AppGallery stores care records locally on your device and does not include account login, advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, HealthKit, or motion permissions.

On iOS, care records are primarily stored locally. Widget summaries use local App Group storage. Purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit, and BabyNova does not receive your payment card details.

AI Care Summaries

When you request an AI care summary in a supported version, BabyNova may send the selected baby's profile and relevant care records to the BabyNova AI service. The service may use configured AI providers such as DeepSeek, Claude, or OpenAI to generate the requested summary.

AI-generated content is for log organization and general information only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Tracking and Advertising

The iOS version uses PostHog and the strict, no-IDFA edition of AppsFlyer to understand app usage and improve the subscription experience. It may send anonymous events such as app opens, paywall views, selected subscription product identifiers, purchase and restore outcomes, product-loading outcomes, and entitlement state. Events may include the app version, build number, platform, and Apple app identifier.

BabyNova does not send baby profile details, feeding, jaundice, weight, diaper or sleep records, care notes, AI prompts or AI results to these analytics services. Session replay and automatic screen or element capture are disabled. BabyNova does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes and does not collect IDFA. The first Huawei AppGallery Android release continues to contain no third-party analytics SDK.

Children's Privacy

BabyNova is intended for parents and caregivers. Please do not enter information unless you have the right to do so.

Data Deletion and Contact

You can delete local records in the app or by uninstalling the app. For privacy questions or deletion requests related to server-side processing, contact us at 68183830@qq.com.
